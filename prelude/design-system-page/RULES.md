# Rules: Design System Page

- **R-s3g.** A project's design system can be read in a page of the app, its gallery as its design session shows it, without opening the session; changing it is its design's.
  *shown-on: /f/:folder/system @ typical*
- **R-n6f.** Reading the design system's gallery never changes the screen its design session shows.
- **R-e2q.** With no design system, or nothing in its gallery yet, the page says so and offers the way to make it.
  *shown-on: /f/:folder/system @ no-system*
