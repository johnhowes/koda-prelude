Tokens Components Guidelines
Theme

Tokens

Prelude’s colour, type, space and shape, each named for its role. Every colour is written once for both themes. Designs use these through the components, and through var() when they need their own rules.

Surfaces and lines

--bgThe page
--surfaceCards, panels, fields
--surface-raisedMenus, dialogs
--surface-sunkenHover, code, wells
--borderDividers, card edges
--border-strongField and button edges

Text

--fgWhat you read: titles, body, labels on controls
--fg-mutedWhat explains: a card’s description, field labels
--fg-subtleWhat annotates: paths, dates, counts

Accent and meaning

--accentThe next step, the current one, links
--accent-hoverThe accent, pointed at
--accent-fgText on the accent
--accent-softYour messages, the current row
--goodIt worked
--good-softBehind good news
--warnNeeds a decision
--warn-softBehind a question
--dangerIt failed; can’t be undone
--danger-softBehind a failure

Series

For telling things apart: a flow’s parts, a chart’s lines. They never mean good, warn or danger, and always have a label beside them. Past five, start again at 1.
