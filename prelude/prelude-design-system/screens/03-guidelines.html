Guidelines

Prelude is a calm workbench: a person and an agent design an app together, and the design is the thing on show. The interface steps back so the work can stand forward. The full text is system/guidelines.md.

Rules

1. One accent, for “this” and “next”

The person should always find the next step at a glance.

DoOne primary button per view: Send, Create design, Start the app build.
Don’tTwo primary buttons side by side, or accent-coloured headings.

2. Name roles, never looks

Both themes, and a host’s own palette, come from the tokens alone.

Docolor: var(--fg-subtle)
Don’tcolor: #888 or padding: 13px

3. Quiet by default; colour means something

If everything is loud, the one failing check isn’t. Colour is never the only signal.

DoBehind main
Don’tA red dot with no words, or green for “nothing happened”.

4. Say what happened, then what to do

The person is often waiting on the agent; every line should move them on.

Do“1 page doesn’t render. The agent is told with your next message.”
Don’t“Error: render failed (exit 1)!”

5. Ask before anything outward or hard to undo

The folder is the person’s; Prelude never surprises them with it.

Do“This pushes prelude/sign-up to origin, where others can see it.” With Not now.
Don’tA Publish button that pushes straight away.

6. Buttons say what they do

A verb and its object, sentence case. The label is the promise.

DoMerge into main · Push and open the pull request · Choose…
Don’tSubmit · OK · Go

7. Components answer to their container

The same component lives in a page, a chat column and a pop-out window. The page’s frame is the app shell’s.

DoContainer queries, flex-wrap, grid.
Don’t@media (max-width: 600px) in a component.

8. Compose pages from layout primitives; space comes only from their gaps

Every block sits inside a .stack, .cluster, .grid, .split or .center, and the space between blocks is that primitive’s gap, from the space scale. Blocks have no outer margins; to space something differently, nest another primitive. One owner for every space means spacing is the same everywhere, and a block moves without bringing stray margins.

Do<div class="stack gap-1"><h3 class="group-label">…<ul class="list">…
Don’tstyle="margin-top: var(--space-4)", or a class that adds margin to a card.

9. Work without script

Views are server-rendered and swapped in place; script is the part that breaks.

Do<details class="menu">
Don’tA click handler that toggles a class.

10. Keyboard and screen reader first

The person may never touch the mouse while talking to the agent.

Do<button class="icon-btn" aria-label="Close">
Don’t<div onclick>, or removing the focus ring.

11. Both themes, always

Prelude follows the person’s system. Check every screen in light and dark (the switch is at the top).

12. What’s pressed says it’s working

A press with no answer reads as a press that didn’t take. Koda marks the link or button pressed (ko-busy) until it’s answered, and the components show it; a page adds nothing. ko-defer only for a region that’s slow on its own, which is rare.

Do
Don’tA “Loading…” label swapped in by script, or ko-defer around a whole page.

Words

Sentence case everywhere, curly apostrophes, and “…” for something in progress or that asks for more.
