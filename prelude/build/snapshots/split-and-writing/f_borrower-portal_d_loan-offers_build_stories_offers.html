See each offer’s monthly payment, and sort offers by cost

Edit

Borrowers compare offers by what they’ll pay each month, so the offers list shows it, and puts the cheapest first.

What changes

  • Each offer in the list shows its monthly payment under the APR.
  • A Sort by menu above the list: Lowest monthly payment (the default), Lowest total cost, Lowest APR.
  • The list keeps the borrower’s choice while they’re on the page, and starts at Lowest monthly payment next time.

Acceptance criteria

  • The monthly payment matches the offer’s details page, to the cent.
  • Offers with the same monthly payment keep their order by APR, lowest first.
  • With one offer, there is no Sort by menu.
  • R-m7t. Offers are listed with the lowest monthly payment first.

Typical, Many offers and Long lender names, each as its approved screen.

  • GET /offers takes ?sort=payment|total|apr ; the list is prelude/loan-offers/views/offers/_list.eta .
  • Each offer gains monthlyPayment .

Pick two offers to compare

Edit

A borrower with two or more offers can tick two and open them side by side.

Depends on: Compare two loan offers side by side (Compare offers).

What changes

  • With two or more offers, each offer in the list has a Compare tick box.
  • Once two are ticked, Compare these two appears above the list and opens Compare offers with them.
  • A third tick isn’t possible until one of the two is unticked.

Acceptance criteria

  • With one offer, or none, there are no tick boxes and no Compare these two.
  • Compare these two opens the two ticked offers, in the order they were ticked.
  • Unticking one hides Compare these two again.

Looks right in

Typical and Many offers, each as its approved screen.

For developers
  • The ticks are a form on GET /offers/compare with a and b ; no new POST.
  • Test data: prelude/loan-offers/states/ ( 01-typical , 02-many-offers ).
