  1. Payment dates on the schedule are a day early in the app. Ana Morales’s loan is due on the 15th of each month, but the schedule lists the 14th: Nov 14, Dec 14, Jan 14. The design shows the 15th.
  2. Read prelude/repayment-schedule/views/schedule/list.eta
  3. Grep dueDate in app/
  4. Read app/lib/dates.js
  5. Read app/views/schedule/list.eta

  6. The cause is in the app, not in the design. formatDate in app/lib/dates.js turns each due date ( 2026-11-15 ) into a Date at midnight UTC, then prints it in the server’s time zone, America/Chicago, where that moment is still the evening of the 14th.

    The design’s states hold the dates as text and print them as they are, which is why its pages show the 15th. The Loan summary’s “Next payment” uses formatDate too, so it’s a day early as well.

    What I’d change: format the date without a time zone in formatDate , and add a test with a date near midnight. Neither design needs to change. Fix it on a branch and I’ll make the change.
